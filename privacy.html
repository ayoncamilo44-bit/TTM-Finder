Privacy Notice

This jurisdiction-neutral draft must be completed with the actual entity name, vendors, retention periods, and regional rights before launch.

1. Information we may collect

Depending on the features we enable, TTM Finder may collect account details, submission content, moderation decisions, technical logs, and communications sent to our support team. Do not submit private addresses or sensitive personal information.

2. How we use information

We may use information to operate the directory, verify and moderate submissions, respond to requests, protect the service, measure performance, and comply with law. We do not promise that a third-party contact destination will remain current.

3. Sharing

We may share information with service providers that help host, secure, moderate, or analyze the service, or when required to respond to a valid legal process. We should never publicly display a contributor’s private account details without permission.

4. Choices and requests

Before launch, provide a monitored privacy contact for access, correction, deletion, objection, and takedown requests. Some records may need to be retained for safety, fraud prevention, or legal compliance.

5. Cookies and analytics

List each analytics, advertising, authentication, and hosting provider actually used by the production site. Do not publish placeholder vendors as if they are active.

6. Updates

Show an effective date and update this notice when data practices change. Counsel should review regional requirements before public launch.
