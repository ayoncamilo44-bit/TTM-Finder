Trust and safety

Community Guidelines

The goal is useful collector research without exposing private people or encouraging unwanted contact.

Share only public-facing information

Submit official websites, public business or agency destinations, team or venue addresses, publisher information, and other clearly public contact methods. Never submit private residential addresses, personal phone numbers, personal email addresses, or location data.

Make reports factual

Include what you sent, when you sent it, what came back, and how the outcome was confirmed. Label estimates and personal opinions clearly. Do not invent response rates or claim endorsement.

Respect people and property

No harassment, threats, hate, impersonation, spam campaigns, doxxing, stolen images, or copyrighted material uploaded without permission. Follow event, postal, venue, and signer-specific rules.

Corrections and removal

Report an unsafe, inaccurate, outdated, or infringing record to the site operator. Moderators may hide a record while checking it and should record the reason for material changes.
