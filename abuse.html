Trust and safety

Abuse Prevention & Platform Integrity

TTM Finder is designed for useful, respectful collector research. Attempts to manipulate records or harm people may result in removal, suspension, account termination, or referral to the appropriate authorities.

Prohibited manipulation

  • Submitting false, fabricated, duplicate, or deliberately misleading reports.
  • Creating multiple accounts or coordinating activity to manipulate ratings, response status, visibility, or search results.
  • Using bots, scripts, scraping tools, automated submissions, or traffic flooding without written authorization.
  • Bribing, threatening, impersonating, or pressuring contributors, moderators, signers, organizations, or site staff.
  • Using the directory to target a private person, publish private information, or organize unwanted contact.
  • Uploading malware, malicious links, stolen credentials, or content intended to compromise the service.

How we may respond

Depending on the situation, we may rate-limit activity, hold submissions for review, hide or correct records, remove content, suspend or terminate accounts, preserve relevant logs, contact affected parties, and cooperate with valid legal or safety requests.

Evidence and audit trail

Material records should retain their source, verification date, confidence level, moderation state, and change history. Moderators should document significant decisions and avoid changing a record solely because a contributor is popular or persistent.

Reporting abuse

Before launch, replace this text with a monitored abuse and safety contact. Include the record URL, a concise description of the suspected manipulation or harm, and supporting evidence. Do not send private personal information unless the safety team specifically requests it.

Fair review

Good-faith corrections are not abuse. The operator should provide a reasonable way to appeal a mistaken removal or suspension while reserving the right to act immediately when there is a credible safety, privacy, security, or legal risk.
