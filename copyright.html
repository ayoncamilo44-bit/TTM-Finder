Rights and takedowns

Copyright process

This is a general process outline, not a substitute for counsel or a jurisdiction-specific DMCA policy.

Do not upload what you do not have rights to share

Contributors are responsible for obtaining permission for photos, scans, writing, and other creative material they submit. Facts and public contact destinations should be written in original language rather than copied from another database.

Report an alleged infringement

Before launch, publish a monitored copyright contact. A complete notice should identify the protected work, the allegedly infringing material and its location, the sender’s contact information, a good-faith statement, an accuracy statement, and the required signature.

Review and counter-notice

The operator should promptly review complete notices, limit access where appropriate, notify the contributor when required, and maintain a documented counter-notice process. Counsel should adapt this workflow to the company’s jurisdiction and hosting provider.

Repeat infringement

Maintain a fair, documented repeat-infringer policy. Apply it consistently and preserve records of notices, decisions, and appeals.
