Record quality

Data Provenance & Accuracy Policy

Every production record should have an owner, source, verification date, confidence label, and visible correction path.

Required record fields

Store the signer or organization name, category, public contact method, source URL, source type, date checked, response status, evidence notes, confidence level, moderation state, and change history.

Source hierarchy

Prefer official websites and published business, team, agency, venue, publisher, or institutional information. Community reports may inform a lead, but should not be treated as official confirmation without review.

Freshness

Show when a record was last verified and mark stale records for review. Never present an old address as current without a clear date and confidence label.

Corrections

Provide a monitored correction channel. Preserve prior values in an internal audit log, record the reason for a material change, and temporarily hide unsafe information while it is being checked.
