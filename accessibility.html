Accessibility Statement

This statement describes the intended standard. Audit the production site with assistive technology before launch.

Our goal

TTM Finder aims to provide an accessible research experience for people using keyboards, screen readers, zoom, high contrast, reduced motion, and other assistive technology.

Current practices

Use semantic headings, labeled controls, visible focus states, keyboard-accessible navigation, text alternatives for meaningful visuals, readable contrast, responsive layouts, and a reduced-motion mode.

Feedback
